Questions

Do I need to bring my car in?

No. Most wheels are picked up where the car is parked, at home or work, taken to a shop for repair and brought back. Some lighter cosmetic work is done on site. Your quote says which.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the wheel and the damage, so we quote each job from your photos. Quotes are free.

What should I send for a quote?

A clear photo of each damaged wheel, plus your vehicle year, make and model, the wheel size, and your city. Attach photos in the form below, or email them to support@slocountywheels.com.

Can I send a video?

Yes, by email. Videos are too large for the form, so tap Email a video in the form below, or send it to support@slocountywheels.com. On iPhone, the Mail app offers Mail Drop for large videos; it needs iCloud turned on. The Gmail app sends large files as a Google Drive link automatically.

Can you fix bent, cracked or diamond-cut wheels?

Bent-wheel straightening, crack welding and diamond-cut or CNC-machined faces are specialized work. Send photos and we’ll tell you honestly what’s possible.

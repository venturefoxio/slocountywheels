What we fix at your place.

Cosmetic wheel damage and refinishing, done on-site so your car never leaves your driveway or parking spot.

  • Curb rash and scrapes

    Rim edges chewed up by curbs, repaired and refinished to blend with the rest of the wheel.

  • Scratches and gouges

    Deeper marks on the face or spokes, filled and brought back to a clean finish.

  • Factory color refinishing

    Painted wheels refinished with a color match to the original factory finish.

  • Color changes and custom finishes

    Gloss black, satin, gunmetal, or the finish you have in mind.

  • Lease-return repair

    Fix wheel damage before you turn the lease in.

  • Brake caliper painting

    Calipers painted in the color you choose.

Common questions

Is the repair really done at my place?

Yes. Every service listed under Services is done at your home or workplace, anywhere in San Luis Obispo County.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the wheel and the damage, so we quote each job from your photos. Quotes are free.

What should I send for a quote?

2-3 photos of each damaged wheel, plus your vehicle year, make and model, the wheel size, and your city. Email it all to support@slocountywheels.com.

Can you fix a bent or cracked wheel?

Not on-site. Straightening, crack welding and diamond-cut faces need a machine shop. Send photos and we’ll tell you straight whether yours can be fixed on-site or needs a machine shop.

Can you fix my wheels before a lease return?

Yes. Send photos of the damage and we’ll quote the repair so the wheels are sorted before you turn the car in.
